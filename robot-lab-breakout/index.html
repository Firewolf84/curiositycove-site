The short versionRobot Lab Breakout collects no personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and it never sends anything about you or your child over the internet.

Privacy policy

Robot Lab Breakout is a code-breaking puzzle game for children aged 9 to 12, made by Curiosity Cove, a small family business in South Africa. This policy covers the Robot Lab Breakout app on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

What the app stores

  • Game progress: which levels are finished, stars, badges, best times and hints used.
  • Leaderboard nicknames: if a player chooses to add one after finishing a level. The app asks players to use a nickname, not their real name.

All of this is saved only on the device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect names, email addresses, locations, photos, contacts or device identifiers.
  • It does not contain advertising, third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not need an internet connection and makes no network requests while you play.
  • It has no in-app chat, no links out of the app and no way to contact other players.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it is designed to meet children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the EU GDPR and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Deleting data

To remove everything the app has saved, delete the app from the device. A grown-up can also use "Reset the game" in the Grown-ups' corner to clear progress, and remove leaderboard names there too.

Changes

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Support

Where is the Grown-ups' corner?

At the bottom of the Play page. Answer the sum to open it. There you can see the answers for the current level, unlock every level, remove leaderboard names, or reset the game.

We're stuck on a puzzle

Every lock has two hints. The second hint nearly gives the method away. Answers are in the Grown-ups' corner.

How do stars work?

One star for finishing a level, two for finishing before the timer runs out, and three for finishing with no hints in 60% of the time.

Can we play on two devices?

Yes, but progress is saved on each device separately, because the app sends nothing over the internet.

Contact us

For help, feedback or a puzzle you think is wrong, email us at the support address shown on the app's page in the App Store or Google Play. We read every message.
